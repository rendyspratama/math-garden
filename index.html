📘 How Math Garden teaches
  • Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract (Singapore Math): tap and count pictures first, then a number bond, then the number sentence.
  • Number bonds: “two parts make the whole” (3 and 2 make 5).
  • Ten-frames & making ten: teen numbers are “10 and some more”.
  • Montessori colours: green squares are ones, blue bars are tens.
  • Three-period lesson: Learn (“This is 7”) → Find it (“Show me 7”) → How many?
  • × is equal groups (3 × 2 = 2 + 2 + 2). ÷ is fair sharing, one at a time. At most 5 groups or friends.
  • Mistakes are never called wrong: the owl checks together with your child.
